Our story

Started with one shelf of plants and a second-hand espresso machine.

Greenleaf opened in 2016 in a narrow shopfront that used to sell bicycles. We kept the tall windows, added more plants than was reasonable, and started roasting on weekends. Ten years on, the bikes are gone but the light still falls the same way across the counter every morning.

We work directly with three farms — in Yirgacheffe, Huila, and up in the Nilgiris — and change the pour-over list as each harvest comes in.
